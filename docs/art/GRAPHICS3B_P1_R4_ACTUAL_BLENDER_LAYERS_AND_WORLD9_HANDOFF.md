## Actual Blender 3D geometry layer separation

The reviewed original 640×640 **orthographic** Blender camera is held at `orthoScale=18.0`. A dedicated native Blender 4.5.14 script calls **real evaluated `Scene.ray_cast`** for every pixel using the original camera geometry, and classifies the closest genuine 3D mesh into:

1. `walls`: building frame, door/window glass, real front and rear gable infills and wooden joints.
2. `foreground`: original forge, hearth, anvil, stone apron, toolbench, hammer, tongs and separated crate.
3. `roof`: original immutable roof tile meshes, genuine Quaternius roof chimney and its actual source-attached smoke wisps.

The resulting 640×640 class mask is saved. The exact original approved day/dusk pixel RGBA is **partitioned, not re-painted or synthetically rendered**, into six mutually disjoint lossless WebPs in `assets/environment/greyhaven/blacksmith-p1/review-only/r4-layers/`. Native Python/Pillow verification **recomposes all three passes into RGBA that is byte-identical with both owner-approved original Blender PNGs**. The full original day+dusk PNGs, raycast audit/visibility counts, layer WebPs and source SHA256 manifest are retained under this *review-only* directory.

The raycast image classification includes genuine non-mesh lighting/shadow pixels; these are placed in the wall/background pass. The exact missing-geometry fallback count and proportion are checked rather than claiming they were ray hits. In the original proof: 12,229 wall, 34,570 foreground, 32,144 roof source raycast hits and 330,657 no mesh on the full frame. The final nontransparent source pixel allocation is recorded in the manifest, including 3,481 non-mesh fallback pixels per two images. No original roof/model/texture was edited.

**This is genuine source-based 2.5D layer production, not tested original online-game 960×540 camera placement.** The live client cannot load these by default; the files reside exclusively under `review-only`. No fake player, world authority or game screenshot is asserted. Material smoke rendered in original Blender source is not an in-game emitter.
